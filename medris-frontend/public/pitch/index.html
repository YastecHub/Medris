Sustainability strategy
08

Built to last: economic, operational, environmental.

Economic

  • Recurring, aligned revenue: commission only on completed sales, or a flat monthly listing fee.
  • Lean costs: a student team on stipends; infrastructure under ₦150K a month in Year 1.
  • Break-even in month 19, with a funded runway and a 36% cash buffer.

Operational

  • Trust built in: vendor verification, ratings, dispute process, order records.
  • Self-running workflows: reminders, auto-cancel, auto-complete, payment matching.
  • Compliance: Terms, Privacy and Refund policies updated Oct 2026; NDPA-aware data handling.

Environmental & social

  • Rent, resell, reuse: rentals and second-hand listings extend product life.
  • Hyperlocal buying: distance-sorted results mean shorter trips and fewer parcels.
  • Student income: small vendors earn online, without a shop or ads budget.
